## Design Context

### Users
Small private households with 2 to 5 active members. They use the app primarily on phones at home or while shopping and need to check the shared grocery list quickly without extra coordination.

### Brand Personality
Warm, practical, reassuring. The interface should feel like a dependable household tool rather than a corporate dashboard or a generic productivity demo.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode-first, mobile-first household utility with warm neutrals, earthy accents, and tactile rounded surfaces. The grocery list should feel calm and clear under supermarket pressure. Avoid cold grayscale UI, SaaS card spam, and purple-blue AI gradients.

### Design Principles
- Make the grocery list and add-item flow visually dominant.
- Keep interactions thumb-friendly and low-friction on phones.
- Use color to communicate action, state, and risk rather than decoration.
- Separate destructive or shared-household actions from everyday actions.
- Maintain one visual system across auth, grocery, favorites, and household screens.
